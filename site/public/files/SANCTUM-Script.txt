SLIDE 01 — HOOK (Aarav) · ~40s
────────────────────────────────
Good morning. We're Team CCA — Aarav, Jeehan, and Anuj — and our project is called SANCTUM.

SANCTUM is a website that pretends not to exist, until you prove you're worthy.

After the Purge every public channel is watched. A rebellion still needs people. So the HCET Syndicate hid the front door inside a dead website — and made finding that door the filter.

────────────────────────────────
SLIDE 02 — PROBLEM (Aarav) · ~35s
────────────────────────────────
Survivors can't google a rebellion.

An open call for recruits is found and burned within hours. So SANCTUM does not advertise. It does not even look like a network. It looks like an abandoned blog.

The Empire sees a dead site. The worthy see the door.

SLIDE 03 — IDEA (Aarav) · ~30s

We built the network.

Two layers. A convincing cover — and the Sanctum behind it. The cover is not a splash screen. It is a complete website. Discovery is the product.

The Seal. The Gate. The Trials. The Sanctum.
