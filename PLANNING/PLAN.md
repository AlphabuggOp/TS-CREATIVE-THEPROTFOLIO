# THE BETTER PLAN — SANCTUM as a showcase, not a document

The previous plan listed 22 sections in a row. That is a case-study blog. A judge who just sat through an 8-minute pitch does not want the pitch again. They want a **place** that holds the work.

## What this site is for

End of the pitch: QR. Not a Drive folder.

- **20 seconds on a projector:** it has to look like SANCTUM.
- **2 minutes for a tired judge:** open the lives, watch the promo, take the files.
- **Wander:** enter chambers. Story is there. It is not the front door.

## What stays (the metaphor)

Decoy title card → three knocks → `0528` → Seal → iris.

If that is missing, the portfolio has failed the assignment. The previous build already has this. We keep it.

## What changes (the showcase)

After the Seal you are not on a long page. You are in **THE HANGAR**.

The hangar is a gallery floor. Seven exhibits, hung like work:

| # | Exhibit | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **THE LIVES** | Browser frames of both decoys. Click = leave for the live site. |
| 02 | **THE THEATER** | The promo, playing in-page. |
| 03 | **THE VAULT** | Every Drive file, actually downloadable. |
| 04 | **THE DOSSIER** | PDFs in a reader + the real write-ups. |
| 05 | **THE RITE** | How the door works. Two decoys. Playable knocks. Four acts. Three trials. |
| 06 | **THE COUNCIL** | Aarav, Jeehan, Anuj. |
| 07 | **THE FORGE** | Stack, tokens, both repos. |

Click an exhibit → the chamber iris-opens (same motion as the Seal). ESC or the wordmark returns you to the floor.

## What we will not do

- Invent a database. The product is static and remembers on-device. So does this.
- Invent bios, screenshots, or quotes. Copy comes from the PDFs and the live sites.
- Build a second dead blog as the decoy. The title card *is* the disguise.
- Twenty-two chapters of prose.

## Why this is more SANCTUM

The app is a hub with chambers. The deck is twelve rooms. The portfolio is now a hangar with exhibits. Same grammar. The work is the show.

Replace the long `Archive` scroll with the hangar + chambers. Keep decoy, seal, files, frames, viewers.
