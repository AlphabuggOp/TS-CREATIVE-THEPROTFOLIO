# PART 2 — WHAT PROJECT SANCTUM IS (study this deeply)
## 2.1 — The one-line pitch
> *"A website that pretends not to exist, until you prove you're worthy."*
## 2.2 — The story
Set in Star Wars, after the Purge (Order 66). The Jedi are gone. The Empire surveils every public channel. The survivors — Force-sensitives and their sympathizers, organized as the **HCET Syndicate** — must recruit new members in total secrecy.
The problem: a rebellion needs people, but any open call for recruits gets found and burned within hours.
The answer: **SANCTUM** — a secret network that **hides its front door in plain sight as a dead, boring website.** The network doesn't advertise. It doesn't even look like a network. It looks like an abandoned blog. And the door itself — the act of discovering it — is the filter. Only someone paying real attention (or someone worthy) finds their way in.
## 2.3 — The mechanics (what the real product does)
- **Two-layer disguise.** The product is literally two websites in one: a convincing abandoned blog (the "cover" / "decoy") and the hidden network (the "Sanctum"). The decoy is not a splash screen — it's a complete, believable, standalone blog.
- **The unlock ritual.** Hidden in the decoy's photo gallery is ONE photo that is wrong — a faint glowing ring. The visitor must:
  1. Notice the wrong photo.
  2. **Click it three times** (each knock adds a static flicker and brightens the ring).
  3. A code field appears: **"THE DOOR ASKS."**
  4. Type the code — which the blog itself quietly teaches you, buried in one of its posts.
  5. The site tears open (static wipe) into the real network.
- **The four acts (inside the Sanctum):**
  - **Act 0 — THE SEAL:** a loader that behaves like establishing a covert uplink. A sigil draws itself; an honest percentage counter runs; status lines decode; at 100% the veil iris-opens.
  - **Act 1 — THE GATE:** 620 viewport-heights of scroll-driven cinematic. You scroll, and you fly — through a portal (a custom GLSL shader) into hyperspace.
  - **Act 2 — THE TRIALS:** three *playable* games — **SIGNAL** (memory), **FOCUS** (steadiness), **CHOICE** (judgment) — judged by a voice-guided **Archivist**.
  - **Act 3 — THE SANCTUM:** the hub — a 3D holo-map of beacons/threat zones, a dead-drop cipher (your sigil is the key), an A* safe-lane router, archives, a council war-room, and a **Beacon** that forges recruitment posters.
- **The Ceremony:** your score (0–9) is minted into a **Rank** (Youngling/Padawan/Knight), a **Callsign**, and a **Sigil** forged from how you played. All persisted.
- **The panic key `~`:** press it, and the whole network instantly vanishes back into the dead blog. Security theatre, made literal.

# PART 7 — SANDBOX SURVIVAL GUIDE (read this — it saves hours)
1. **`node_modules` and `/tmp` get wiped at turn boundaries.** Re-run `npm install` whenever a turn can't find deps. Commit everything load-bearing (fonts, browser libs, generated assets).
2. **Non-committed files vanish.** Once you generate showcase screenshots or a build, commit them or they're gone next turn.
3. **Only npm + PyPI + Arena tools reach the network.** Direct `curl`/`git clone` to github.com often fails with a TLS handshake error. Use `fetch_page`/`web_search`/`image_search` for web content.
4. **Headless Chromium (SwiftShader) renders DIMMER than a real GPU.** Your screenshots look darker than the real site. Don't over-brighten; build in a generous contrast margin.
5. **`background-clip: text` + a CSS `filter`/`drop-shadow` BREAKS in headless Chromium** — gradient text renders dark/invisible. For guaranteed-visible big text, use solid bright fill + `text-shadow` halo, NOT gradient-clip. Verify title visibility with a headless screenshot + pixel check.
6. **Don't gate critical logic on `requestAnimationFrame`/animation `onComplete`** — on slow machines rAF can stall. Use wall-clock `setTimeout` for anything that MUST happen (unlock, slide change, loader completion).
7. **Git is session-locked to one `arena/…` branch.** When the PR merges, remote access ends — finish pushes BEFORE the user merges.
8. **An Arena `commit-msg` hook may auto-append `Co-authored-by: arena-agent`.** At the very end, remove `.git/hooks/commit-msg` and squash to one clean commit (zero AI trace for judges).
9. **Vite dev server:** set `server.host: true` + `allowedHosts: true` in `vite.config` or the preview host is rejected.
10. **Fonts:** self-host via `@fontsource`. For Python (Pillow) image work, only system fonts (DejaVu) exist unless you bundle a TTF.
